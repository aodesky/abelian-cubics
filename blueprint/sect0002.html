2 The orbit parametrization

A \(C_3\)-algebra is either a cyclic cubic field with a specified \(C_3\)-action, or \(\mathbb {Q}^3\) with cyclic permutation of its factors. An element is normal when its three conjugates form a \(\mathbb {Q}\)-basis. We identify pairs \((K,x)\) up to \(C_3\)-equivariant \(\mathbb {Q}\)-algebra isomorphism taking one distinguished element to the other.

Theorem 5 Rational points of the moduli space

Lean declarations

  • CubicTargets.OrbitParametrization.ModularInterpretation.Claim
  • Cubic.OrbitParametrization.C3Algebra.«thm:modularInterpretation»

There is a bijection between rational points of \(T\) and equivalence classes of \(C_3\)-algebras \(K/\mathbb {Q}\) equipped with a trace-one normal element \(x\in K\).

Proof in Lean ▼

Construct the algebra and normal element from a rational quotient point, and recover the quotient point from the conjugates of the normal element. These constructions are inverse on isomorphism classes. This node states the rational-point part of the paper’s theorem.

Example 1
✓
#

Lean declarations

  • CubicTargets.OrbitParametrization.TwistedCubicField.Claim
  • Cubic.OrbitParametrization.C3Algebra.«example:twoRationalPointsSamePoly»

Let \(K\) be a cubic abelian number field. Then \(K\), equipped with its canonical Galois action, is a \(C_3\)-algebra. The twist \(K'\) of the \(C_3\)-algebra \(K\) by the outer automorphism \(g \mapsto g^{-1}\) of \(C_3\) (with twisted action \(g \ast x = g^{-1}x\)) is not isomorphic to \(K\) as a \(C_3\)-algebra. 1

Proof in Lean ▼

The Galois group of a cyclic cubic field is abelian. An isomorphism to the inverse-action twist would therefore force a generator to equal its inverse, a contradiction. The cocycles describe the two rational points.

Example 2

  • CubicTargets.OrbitParametrization.SplitAlgebraTwist.Claim
  • Cubic.OrbitParametrization.C3Algebra.«example:splitAlgebra»

Let \(K_{\text{spl}} = \mathbb {Q}^3\), the split cubic algebra. Then \(C_3\subset S_3 = \mathrm{Aut}_{\mathbb {Q}\text{-alg}}(K_{\text{spl}})\) and \(K_{\text{spl}}\), equipped with its canonical \(C_3\)-action, is a \(C_3\)-algebra. Any transposition gives an isomorphism of \(C_3\)-algebras from \(K_{\text{spl}}\) to its twist \(K_{\text{spl}}'\).

Proof in Lean ▼

Every algebra automorphism of \(\mathbb {Q}^3\) permutes its three coordinate idempotents. A transposition conjugates cyclic translation to its inverse, giving the asserted isomorphism with the twist.

Example 3
✓

Lean declarations

  • CubicTargets.OrbitParametrization.SplitNormalElements.Claim
  • Cubic.OrbitParametrization.C3Algebra.«example:splitAlgebraNormalElement»

An element \(x\) of trace one in the split \(C_3\)-algebra \(K_{\text{spl}}\) is normal if and only if \(x\) either has distinct coordinates or exactly two identical coordinates. For such a normal element \(x\), the pairs \((K_{\text{spl}},x)\) and \((K_{\text{spl}}',x)\) are equivalent if and only if \(x\) has exactly two identical coordinates (swapping the identical coordinates gives the required isomorphism); in particular, if \(x\) has distinct coordinates then \((K_{\text{spl}},x)\) and \((K_{\text{spl}}',x)\) determine different rational points of \(\mathcal G_1/C_3\), even though \(K_{\text{spl}}\) and \(K_{\text{spl}}'\) are isomorphic as \(C_3\)-algebras.

Proof in Lean ▼

Evaluate the explicit algebra and cyclic action, then compute the characteristic polynomial and the normality condition.

  1. In terms of Galois cohomology, the non-cohomologous \(1\)-cocycles in \(H^1(\mathbb {Q},C_3)\) corresponding to the \(C_3\)-algebras \(K\) and \(K'\) have the same image under the canonical map \(H^1(\mathbb {Q},C_3) \to H^1(\mathbb {Q},S_3)\) because the outer automorphism of \(C_3\) is realized by \(S_3\)-conjugation.
