10 Shared supporting lemmas

These lemmas occur inside several of the Lean proofs. Showing them as separate nodes makes their shared use visible in the dependency graph. Their numbering is separate from the statements of the paper.

Supporting lemma 1 Inverse splitting coordinates
✓
#

Lean declarations

  • Cubic.SurfaceGeometry.RationalFormSplitting.inverse_split

For \(r\ne 0\) in a field of characteristic zero, the substitution \(x_1^\prime =x_1+rx_2\), \(x_2^\prime =x_1-rx_2\) has the inverse \(x_1=(x_1^\prime +x_2^\prime )/2\), \(x_2=(x_1^\prime -x_2^\prime )/(2r)\). The linked lemma checks the composition in this direction.

Proof in Lean ▼

Substitute the two inverse expressions into the original coordinates.

Supporting lemma 2 Fibres of the polynomial map
✓
#

Lean declarations

  • Cubic.QuadraticCorrespondence.QuotientPolynomialConverse.separable_fibre_card

Let \(f=t^3-t^2+at+b\in \mathbb {Q}[t]\) be separable, and suppose that \(f\) either has Galois group \(C_3\) or splits over \(\mathbb {Q}\). Exactly two rational torus points have characteristic polynomial \(f\).

Proof in Lean ▼

Use the two roots of the quadratic resolvent and their distinctness in the separable case.

Supporting lemma 6 Character evaluations in idempotent coordinates
✓
#

Lean declarations

  • Cubic.ToricHeights.FormulaForNormCharacter.idempotent_values

Over a field of characteristic zero containing a primitive cube root \(\zeta \), write \(g=u e_0+v e_1+w e_2\) in the idempotent basis of the group algebra. The three character evaluations are \(u,v,w\):

\[ g_0+g_1+g_2=u,\qquad g_0+\zeta g_1+\zeta ^2g_2=v,\qquad g_0+\zeta ^2g_1+\zeta g_2=w. \]
Proof in Lean ▼

Evaluate the three characters on the three idempotents.

Supporting lemma 7 The difference of the cubic characters
✓
#

Lean declarations

  • Cubic.ToricHeights.WeightVectors.eigenvalue_cubes_difference

In a commutative ring, if \(\zeta ^2+\zeta +1=0\), then

\[ (x+y\zeta +z\zeta ^2)^3-(x+y\zeta ^2+z\zeta )^3=3(2\zeta +1)(x-z)(y-x)(z-y). \]
Proof in Lean ▼

Expand the cubes and use the relation satisfied by \(\zeta \).

Supporting lemma 8 Local height from the weights

Supporting lemma 11 The finite adelic logarithm
✓
#

Lean declarations

  • Cubic.PoissonSummation.FiniteAdelicData.logarithm_eq_sum

If an adele has component in the maximal compact subgroup at every prime outside a finite set \(S\), its finite logarithm is the sum of the local logarithms over \(S\).

Proof in Lean ▼

The logarithm vanishes on each maximal compact subgroup, so all other terms vanish.

Supporting lemma 13 Evaluation of the archimedean integral
✓
#

Lean declarations

  • Cubic.PoissonSummation.ArchimedeanFourier.fourier_eq_fraction

For \(\operatorname {Re}(s_0){\gt}0\) and \(\operatorname {Re}(\sigma ){\gt}0\), the Fourier integral in the logarithm coordinate, with Lebesgue measure, is

\[ \frac{s_0+2\sigma }{(s_0+2\pi it)(2\sigma -2\pi it)}. \]
Proof in Lean ▼

Integrate separately on the two half-lines and combine the resulting fractions.

Supporting lemma 14 Convergence of the split correction factors
✓

For \(\operatorname {Re}(z){\gt}1/2\),

\[ \sum _{p\equiv 1\pmod3}\left|\left(1-3p^{-2z}+2p^{-3z}\right)-1\right|{\lt}\infty . \]
