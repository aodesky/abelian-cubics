Cited theorem 3Kühleitner–Nowak

Let \(a_K(n)\) count integral ideals of norm \(n^3\) in a fixed quadratic field \(K\). If \(F\) is analytic at \(1\) and agrees with \((s-1)^2\sum _{n\geq 1}a_K(n)n^{-s}\) to its right, then

\[ \sum _{n\leq X}a_K(n)=F(1)X\log X+(F^\prime (1)-F(1))X+O\bigl(\sqrt X(\log X)^3(\log \log X)^2\bigr). \]

This is the residue form of [ 21 , Corollary 2 ] . The removal of the factor at \(3\) and the comparison with the original count are proved in Lean.

Example 3

An element \(x\) of trace one in the split \(C_3\)-algebra \(K_{\text{spl}}\) is normal if and only if \(x\) either has distinct coordinates or exactly two identical coordinates. For such a normal element \(x\), the pairs \((K_{\text{spl}},x)\) and \((K_{\text{spl}}',x)\) are equivalent if and only if \(x\) has exactly two identical coordinates (swapping the identical coordinates gives the required isomorphism); in particular, if \(x\) has distinct coordinates then \((K_{\text{spl}},x)\) and \((K_{\text{spl}}',x)\) determine different rational points of \(\mathcal G_1/C_3\), even though \(K_{\text{spl}}\) and \(K_{\text{spl}}'\) are isomorphic as \(C_3\)-algebras.

If \(t^3-t^2+at+b\in \mathbb {Z}[t]\) has Galois group \(C_3\), then \(a\leq 0\). Fix \(a \in \mathbb {Z}_{\leq 0}\). The number of polynomials of the form \(t^3 -t^2 + at + b \in \mathbb {Z}[t]\) with Galois group \(C_3\) is equal to

\begin{equation*} \frac12\sum _{d|(1-3a)} 3^{\omega (Q_1(d))} (-1)^{\Omega (Q_2(d))} -\frac16 \# E_{\sqrt{1-3a}}(\mathbb {Z}) \end{equation*}

where \(Q_j(d)\) denotes the largest divisor of \(d\) only divisible by primes \(\equiv j \pmod3\), and \(\omega (n)\) (resp. \(\Omega (n)\)) denotes the number of prime factors of a positive integer \(n\) counted without (resp. with) multiplicity.

Theorem 1All monic cubics

Theorem 6The multivariate height zeta function

Fix any \(s \in (\mathbb {C}^{\Sigma (1)})^{\Gamma }\) with \(\mathrm{Re}(s_0+s_1+s_2) {\gt} 2\). Then the multivariate Dirichlet series

\begin{equation*} Z(s)= \sum _{\substack {P \in T(\mathbb Q)\\ \begin{bgroup} \text{\emph{integral}} \end{bgroup}}} H(P,s)^{-1} \end{equation*}

is absolutely convergent and equals

\begin{align*} \left({1-3^{-z}}\right) \zeta (z) \prod _{q\equiv 2\pmod{3}} \left(1+\frac{1}{q^{z}}\right)^{-1} \prod _{p\equiv 1\pmod{3}} \left(1+\frac{3}{p^z}\left(1-\frac{1}{p^z}\right)^{-1}\right) \end{align*}

where \(z = \frac{1}{2}(s_0+s_1+s_2)\). This multivariate Dirichlet series admits a meromorphic continuation to \(\{ s \in (\mathbb {C}^{\Sigma (1)})^{\Gamma } : \mathrm{Re}(s_0+s_1+s_2) {\gt} 1\} \).
